# Failed or pending payments

A failed payment did not complete and does not increase the invoice's paid balance. A pending payment has been submitted but is not confirmed yet. Review the payment status and retry only after checking that no pending attempt exists.

If retries continue to fail, verify the payment method, billing address, and available funds. Support can investigate using the invoice ID and payment timestamp.
