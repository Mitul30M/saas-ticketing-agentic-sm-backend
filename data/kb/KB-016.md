# Nimbus trials and first billing

Trial workspaces show the trial end date in Billing settings. If a payment method is present, the selected plan may begin billing when the trial ends. The invoice and subscription records determine whether billing started.

Ask support to review an unexpected first invoice using its invoice ID.
