# Nimbus plans and included features

Nimbus offers Free, Starter, Pro, and Business plans. Feature availability and limits vary by plan. The current plan and subscription record are the source of truth for a workspace.

Contact an authorized workspace admin before changing a plan. Support can explain available plans but cannot change a subscription without approval.
