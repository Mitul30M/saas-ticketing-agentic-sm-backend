# Investigating an unexpected invoice amount

Start with the invoice line items and compare the plan, seat count, billing cycle, credits, and taxes with the workspace settings. A mid-cycle plan or seat change can create a prorated charge or credit.

Support can review a disputed amount when given the invoice ID and the specific line item. Never treat an amount claimed only in a message as proof of the amount charged.
