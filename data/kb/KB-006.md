# Nimbus refund policy

Nimbus may refund a successful payment when the request is made within 30 days of the invoice payment date. The refundable amount cannot exceed the amount paid minus any amount already refunded. Failed or pending payments are not refundable.

Refunds are proposals, not automatic changes. Support must verify the sender, invoice ownership, payment status, refund window, remaining balance, and duplicate open requests. A human approver must approve before money is changed.
