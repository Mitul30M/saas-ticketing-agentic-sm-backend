# Invoice due dates and open balances

Monthly invoices are due on the date shown on the invoice. An open invoice means Nimbus has not recorded a successful payment. Check the invoice status before retrying payment, because a pending payment may still settle.

If an invoice remains open after a successful bank or card payment, provide the invoice ID and payment confirmation to support. Do not send card numbers in a ticket.
