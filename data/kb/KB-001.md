# Understanding a Nimbus invoice

Each Nimbus invoice lists the workspace, billing period, plan, seats, taxes, credits, and total. A paid invoice has a recorded payment; an open invoice still needs payment. The invoice ID is the safest reference when contacting support.

## What to check

Compare the billing period and plan with the workspace's subscription. Seat changes can produce a prorated line item. Nimbus displays amounts in USD unless the account contract says otherwise.
