# Upgrading a Nimbus plan

A workspace admin can request an upgrade from Settings, Billing, and Change plan. Nimbus shows the target plan and any prorated charge before confirmation.

An upgrade proposal must identify the current and target plans and its effective date. It remains pending until an authorized human approves it.
