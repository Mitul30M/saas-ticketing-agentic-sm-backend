# Refund eligibility reference

This archived reference described a 14-day refund window for successful Nimbus payments. It is retained to test source recency and must not override the current policy. The current article, payment record, and configured business clock are authoritative.

## Historical rule

Under the older policy, the request had to arrive within 14 days of the successful payment and could not exceed the remaining paid balance. Failed and pending payments were excluded.

## Current handling

Every refund still requires a recorded human approval before execution. Retrieval and tool responses must include the article update date so a newer policy can win when sources overlap.
