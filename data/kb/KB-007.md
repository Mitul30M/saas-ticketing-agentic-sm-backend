# Refund eligibility reference

For a successful Nimbus payment, a refund request is generally eligible during the 30-day period after payment. The requested amount must be no greater than the unrefunded paid amount. The current policy and payment record take precedence over this older reference.

Every refund still requires a recorded human approval before execution.
