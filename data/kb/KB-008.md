# Proration and refund calculations

When a plan changes during a billing period, Nimbus calculates a credit or charge for the unused portion of the old plan and the remaining portion of the new plan. The exact amount is shown as a line item on the invoice.

A refund is limited by the successful payment and prior refunds. Do not estimate a refund from a plan price alone; use the invoice and payment records.
