# Updating a Nimbus payment method

Workspace billing admins can update the payment method from Settings, Billing, and Payment method. The new method is used for future charges; it does not automatically change the status of an already failed invoice.

For safety, do not include full card numbers, security codes, or bank credentials in support messages.
